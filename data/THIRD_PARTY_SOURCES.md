benchmark-authored task data, without copies of these third-party PDFs or images.

BNP Paribas's [official legal information](https://invest.bnpparibas/en/legal-information),
Section 2, describes the rights in its website content and restrictions on
reproduction, distribution, and redistribution. Users must determine whether
their retrieval, use, or redistribution is permitted under the applicable
source terms and law. A publicly accessible source URL is not a redistribution
license.

For multimodal evaluation, the toolkit retrieves a source PDF at runtime and
renders the designated page in memory. It does not write the downloaded PDF or
rendered page to a disk cache. Text-only evaluation receives the source URL and
page description. Neither pathway changes the rights in the source material.

Historical Table 1 evaluations used the original screenshots. The replacement
of screenshots with rendered pages or URL references changes model inputs, so
the URL-reference revision is not guaranteed to reproduce those scores. Source
availability or content can also change at the publisher's discretion.
